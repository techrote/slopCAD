# v5 — dense contoured 13-pocket stand

Current compact version of the hex-bit stand. This combines the contoured 13-pocket form with the dense lattice used by the 51-pocket design.

- Capacity: **13**
- Layout: **4–5–4**
- Functional pocket: **7.0 mm AF**
- Pocket depth: **12.0 mm**
- Base/floor: **1.0 mm**
- Lead-in: **0.6 mm high**, widening to **8.2 mm AF**
- Nearest-neighbour pitch: **8.8 mm**
- Straight-section web: **1.8 mm**
- Minimum top lead-in web: **0.6 mm**
- Overall envelope: approximately **50.6 × 33.02 × 13.0 mm**

## Comfort-lip revision

Compared with the first dense prototype, the full-height lower lip is **1.2 mm taller** and the bottom perimeter has a **0.4 mm × 0.4 mm 45° chamfer**. The overall stand height and pocket geometry remain unchanged.

Designed for support-free printing with a 0.6 mm nozzle.
