## Repository convention

Use `projects/<family>/<variant>/` for distinct models and their source/mesh files; family indexes live at `projects/<family>/README.md`. The prior singular `project/` tree has been consolidated under `projects/` without removing historical CAD. Actual photos of produced parts are preferred as project cover imagery; renders may be included as supplementary design references.
